# Privacy request register

Do not place full identity documents, passwords, health records, payment data, or unnecessary request content in this register. Store restricted supporting material in an approved location and reference it by identifier.

## Operating rules

- Log access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, and objection requests when received.
- Verify identity proportionately without collecting unnecessary data.
- Search every applicable internal system, recipient mailbox, and processor/subprocessor.
- Record legal or contractual retention exceptions and obtain review before denying or limiting a request.
- Retain the response and completion evidence without retaining more requester data than necessary.
- Treat this template as a prospective control record; do not fabricate historical requests or “none received” attestations.
