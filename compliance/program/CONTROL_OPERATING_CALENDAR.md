# SOC 2 control operating calendar

Proposed calendar. Owners and dates remain unapproved until assigned by Fan Works management and aligned with the auditor.

## Continuous / event-driven

- Log security incidents and customer complaints when received.
- Retain access requests and approvals before access is granted.
- Complete joiner/mover/leaver evidence at each workforce change.
- Retain change request, review, test, approval, deployment, and rollback evidence for production changes.
- Triage vulnerability, secret-scanning, uptime, and security alerts against documented service levels.
- Record policy exceptions before implementation, with compensating controls and expiry.

## Monthly

- Review privileged/admin accounts across Comp AI, Google Workspace, GitHub, Azure, Railway, Auth0, Cloudflare, and other in-scope systems.
- Review Azure/hosting alerts and incident tickets; retain reviewer/date/outcome.
- Review vendor changes and new subprocessors.
- Confirm device inventory, encryption, patching, and endpoint-agent freshness.

## Quarterly

- Perform and sign the access review across all in-scope systems.
- Refresh infrastructure and application inventories.
- Review risk register treatments, owners, due dates, and residual risk.
- Review vulnerability backlog and dependency-security posture.
- Review business-continuity contacts and recovery dependencies.
- Review physical access/key/visitor evidence if a physical workplace is in scope.

## Semiannual

- Run an incident-response tabletop with after-action tracking.
- Test a representative backup restoration if the auditor accepts semiannual cadence; otherwise follow the agreed cadence.
- Review critical vendor assurance reports and bridge letters.

## Annual

- Management review of the security program and system scope.
- Policy review, approval, publication, and workforce acknowledgment.
- Security-awareness training and role-specific training.
- Personnel performance/job-description review.
- Penetration test and remediation validation.
- Business-continuity/disaster-recovery exercise.
- Auditor scope, evidence, and sampling-plan refresh.

## Evidence rules

- Record who performed and reviewed the control, the exact date/time, systems/population covered, exceptions found, actions taken, and approval/sign-off.
- Preserve raw exports and hashes; do not overwrite prior-period evidence.
- Keep local, uploaded, reviewed, accepted, and auditor-tested states distinct.
- Never mark a control complete based only on a policy or template.
