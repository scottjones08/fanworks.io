# Incident-response tabletop plan

This is a proposed exercise plan, not evidence that an exercise occurred.

## Scenario

A privileged cloud credential is compromised, an attacker accesses a customer-data workload, and suspicious outbound activity is detected while the primary operator is unavailable.

## Objectives

- Validate detection, severity classification, escalation, containment, evidence preservation, customer/regulatory decision-making, recovery, and executive communication.
- Confirm ownership and alternate contacts for Azure, GitHub, Railway, Auth0, Google Workspace, Cloudflare, and Comp AI.
- Verify that the team can revoke credentials, preserve logs, assess data impact, and restore service without destroying evidence.
